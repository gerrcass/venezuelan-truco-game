# Venezuelan Truco — the rules this mesa plays

This is the written authority for **Truco e' Piedra**. It follows the Venezuelan game described by Wikipedia and Ludoteka, then resolves the places where real tables disagree. If the code and this document disagree, the code is wrong.

**Sources**

- Wikipedia, *Truco venezolano* — <https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truco_venezolano>
- Ludoteka, *Reglas del Truco Venezolano* — <https://www.ludoteka.com/juegos/truco-venezolano/reglas>
- El Truco Venezolano, *Vocabulario* — <https://eltrucovenezolano.com/vocabulario/>

## 1. The deck

A Spanish 40-card deck: the four palos **oro, copa, espada, basto**, each with **1–7, 10 (sota), 11 (caballo), 12 (rey)**. No 8s, 9s or comodines.

### Vira, perico and perica

After the deal, one card is turned face up under the deck: the **vira**. Its palo decides the two pieces:

- **Perico** — the **caballo (11)** of the vira's palo. If the vira itself is a caballo, the perico is the **rey (12)** of that palo.
- **Perica** — the **sota (10)** of the vira's palo. If the vira itself is a sota, the perica is the **rey (12)** of that palo.

A vira of 1–7 or 12 leaves perico = 11 and perica = 10 of its palo. The vira is not in anybody's hand.

### Card hierarchy, strongest to weakest

| Rank | Card(s) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Perico** |
| 2 | **Perica** |
| 3 | **1 de espada** (espadilla) |
| 4 | **1 de basto** (bastillo) |
| 5 | **7 de espada** |
| 6 | **7 de oro** |
| 7 | **3** (all palos) |
| 8 | **2** (all palos) |
| 9 | **1 de oro**, **1 de copa** (ases falsos) |
| 10 | **12** (reyes), except when it is the perico or perica |
| 11 | **11** (caballos), except when it is the perico |
| 12 | **10** (sotas), except when it is the perica |
| 13 | **7 de copa**, **7 de basto** (sietes falsos) |
| 14 | **6** (all palos) |
| 15 | **5** (all palos) |
| 16 | **4** (all palos) |

Cards on the same row tie. A tie is a **parda**.

## 2. The reparto

1. The **pie** (dealer) shuffles, offers the cut, and deals three cards to each player, one at a time, starting with the **mano**.
2. The next card is turned face up as the **vira** and left under the deck.
3. The **mano** plays first. Play is counterclockwise. Partners sit opposite each other.
4. The winner of a baza leads the next. If a baza is parda, the player who led it leads the next (house ruling).
5. The pie rotates counterclockwise after every reparto.

In this mesa the seats are: **you** (south), **El Catire** (east), **El Pana** (north, your partner), **La Comadre** (west). The first reparto has pie = La Comadre, so **you are mano**.

## 3. Bazas and the reparto

Each player plays one card per baza. The highest card wins the baza. A reparto is won by the team that wins **two of the three bazas**.

Ties:

- If the first baza is parda, the second decides; if the second is also parda, the third decides.
- If both teams have won one baza and the third is parda, the team that won the **first** baza wins the reparto.
- If all three bazas are parda, the **mano's team** wins.
- Operationally: the first team to win a baza wins any unresolved finish; with no winner at all, the mano's team wins.

**Quemar** (burning): instead of playing a card face up, you may play it face down. A burned card has no rank: every face-up card beats it, and it ties only another burned card. Burning still counts as your card for the baza and closes your flor/envido window.

## 4. Envido

Envido is a bet on which team holds the better **tanto**. It may be called only before the first card of the reparto, on your turn.

### Tanto

Only the three cards in a hand count.

- Face values: 1–7 are nominal; **10, 11 and 12 are worth 0**.
- With **perico**: **30 + the highest face value of the other two cards**.
- With **perica**: **29 + the highest face value of the other two cards**.
- Otherwise, with two cards of the same palo: **20 + their face values**. With three of a palo, use the two highest.
- With three different palos and no piece: the **highest face value** among the three.

Examples (vira = espada, so perico = 11 de espada, perica = 10 de espada):

- 11 de espada + 5 de oro + 4 de copa → **35** (30 + 5)
- 10 de espada + 7 de basto + 6 de basto → **36** (29 + 7; a piece counts with the highest of the other two, not with both)
- 7 de oro + 6 de oro + 4 de copa → **33** (20 + 7 + 6)
- 12 de copa + 3 de basto + 1 de oro → **3** (figures are 0)

A team's envido is the **better tanto of its two players**. Maximum is 37 (perico + a 7).

### The envido ladder

| Canto | Querido | No querido |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Envido** | 2 piedras | caller takes 1 |
| **Quiero y envido** | 4 | caller takes 2 |
| **Quiero y 5 piedras más** | 7 | caller takes 4 |
| **Envido la falta** | the leader's remaining distance to 24 | caller takes 7 |

The team that accepts may raise; the original caller then answers. Once accepted, the envido is compared **at the end of the reparto**, after the last baza or after a fold/decline. The winner takes the accepted stake; ties go to the mano's team. **La falta** is fixed when it is accepted: the leader's distance to 24 at that moment, never less than one piedra. Later points do not change it.

A **declined** envido is recorded but not paid immediately. It settles at the end of the reparto, so a flor declared before then can still cancel it (see §5). Declining pays the caller team 1, 2, 4 or 7 piedras for levels 1–4.

### Flor cancels envido

If any player declares **flor** before the envido settles, the envido is cancelled. A flor may be declared on your own turn or while you are answering an envido or truco; a pending envido is cancelled immediately, while a pending truco stays on the table for you to answer. No envido points are paid. If both teams have flor, see §5.

### Prive

When a team is **one piedra from winning** (23), **that team** may call **prive** before the first card, if no envido or flor has been called. Both teams' tantos are compared: the higher takes **1 piedra**; ties go to the mano's team. The other team's tanto is the answer. Prive replaces envido for that reparto. It is awarded immediately and can end the partida; if it reaches 24, the reparto ends there and no truco payout is made.

## 5. Flor

A hand has **flor** when:

- all three cards are the same palo; or
- it holds **perico or perica plus two cards of one palo** (the two may be a different palo from the piece); or
- it holds **perico and perica**.

**Flor** is worth **3 piedras**. **Flor reservada** (perico + perica) is worth **5 piedras**, is automatic, and beats every other flor.

### Flor por derecho

This mesa plays **flor por derecho**. You must declare flor **before playing your first card** of the reparto. If you play a card first, the flor is lost. The declaration can be made on your turn, or while you are answering an envido or truco, and it cancels envido immediately (a pending envido is dismissed; a pending truco still needs an answer).

If both teams declare flor, the best flor of each team is compared; a team only scores its flor once. **Flor reservada** beats every numeric flor; otherwise the higher numeric wins; a numeric tie goes to the mano's team. The winner's team takes 3 (or 5 for reservada), the loser takes nothing. **This mesa does not play the optional flor-envido betting subgame**; the comparison is automatic. If only one team declares, it simply takes its flor points, and the other team's undeclared flor is forfeited.

Flor values:

- three of a palo: **20 + the three face values**
- perico/perica + two of a palo: **30/29 + the two face values**
- flor reservada: **5 piedras**, not a numeric tanto

**When the flor settles (house ruling).** A declaration is announced immediately and shows a pending **+3** (or **+5**) on the table, but the points settle **when the first baza closes**: after the first trick is recorded and before the second baza begins. If the reparto ends earlier — a fold, a declined truco, or a match — the flor settles in that final accounting. Settling at the first baza close gives both teams the whole first baza to declare, so the comparison is never decided by play order. A flor that reaches 24 ends the partida immediately, and the truco payout is not made.

## 6. Truco

The truco is the bet on the reparto itself. It can be called on your turn, before playing a card, in any baza, as long as no call is pending and the ladder has not topped out.

| Canto | Querido | No querido |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Truco** | 3 piedras | caller takes 1 |
| **Retruco** | 6 | caller takes 3 |
| **Vale nueve** | 9 | caller takes 6 |
| **Vale juego** | the reparto decides the partida | caller takes 9 |

- If nobody calls truco, the winning team takes **1 piedra**.
- The team that accepts a call may raise it; the other team answers.
- A declined truco ends the reparto immediately; the caller's team takes the previous stake. Any accepted envido is still compared first.
- **Irse al mazo** (fold) concedes the reparto: the opposing team takes the current stake (1 if no truco was accepted). A fold does not erase an accepted envido.

## 7. Scoring order

At the end of a reparto, every point is itemized in the hand summary:

1. Flor settles when the first baza closes, or at hand end if the reparto ends earlier. If it reaches 24, the partida ends immediately; nothing later is paid.
2. Prive is paid immediately when it is called. If it reaches 24, the partida ends immediately.
3. Compare any accepted envido, or pay a declined one, and settle it. If it reaches 24, the partida ends immediately; the truco payout is not made.
4. Check for 24.
5. Pay the truco stake (or 1 if no truco) to the reparto winner, or the decline/fold value.
6. Check for 24 again.

A team wins the moment it reaches **24 piedras**. There is no win-by-two. The result explains which lance closed the reparto: bazas, a decline, a fold, envido, flor or prive.

## 8. Señas

Partners may signal. This mesa gives you **one seña per reparto**, from El Pana:

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| guiño del ojo derecho | perico |
| guiño del ojo izquierdo | perica |
| lengua a la derecha | espadilla |
| lengua a la izquierda | bastillo |
| punta de lengua a la derecha | 7 de espada |
| punta de lengua a la izquierda | 7 de oro |
| inflar los cachetes | flor |
| guiño izquierdo y luego derecho | flor reservada |

If El Pana has none of these, he shrugs. The seña is a hint, not a rule; it never changes the cards.

## 9. Table talk

These are flavor in this game, not mechanics:

- **A ley** — "there may or may not be flor." A bluff.
- **Barajo** — the cry for a misdeal or an exposed piece. There are no misdeals in a digital shuffle.
- **Son buenas** — conceding an envido without showing your tanto.
- **Ven a mí / Quédate** — asking a partner to play low/high.
- **¿Llevas?** — asking a partner whether they have a good envido.

## 10. House rulings (the short version)

| Topic | Real-table variation | This mesa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match target | 12 or 24 | **24** |
| Flor reservada | 5 (Wikipedia) or 3 (some tables) | **5** |
| Both teams have flor | optional flor-envido bets | **automatic comparison** of each team's best flor; higher takes 3/5; numeric tie to mano |
| Flor declaration | optional or flor por derecho | **flor por derecho**, before your first card; also while answering a canto |
| Flor settlement | immediate at the declaration | **when the first baza closes** (or at hand end if earlier), so both teams can declare |
| Envido as a response to truco | allowed at some tables | **only on your own turn before the first card** |
| Declined envido | paid at once or at hand end | **settled at hand end**; a flor before then cancels it |
| Envido la falta | varies | **fixed at acceptance**: the leader's distance to 24 then |
| Prive | some tables, either team | **the team at 23 calls**, the other team's tanto answers; 1 piedra; replaces envido |
| Quemar | common | **implemented**; a bot burns at most once per reparto |
| A ley / barajo | table calls | **flavor only** |
| Parda leader | varies | **the player who led the tied baza leads next** |
| Envido revealed | loser may say "son buenas" | **both tantos shown at resolution** for teaching |

## 11. What "winning" looks like

The first team to **24 piedras** wins the partida. The scoreboard shows both teams' stones, the current stake, the vira and the running log. At the end, the table offers a rematch with the same personas and a fresh shuffle.
