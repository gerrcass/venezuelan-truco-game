# Four-player partnerships at 24 piedras

Venezuelan truco is usually played by four players in two fixed pairs, and the partnership is where señas, table talk and half the strategy live. We are building a single-player game, so a 2v2 table with three bots preserves that social texture in a way 1v1 cannot. The partida runs to 24 piedras rather than 12 so the full betting ladder, the prive at 23 and the comeback arc are all reachable in a normal session. The trade-off is more AI and turn-management complexity, accepted deliberately.

**Consequences:** seats are fixed, partners sit opposite, play is counterclockwise, and the first reparto makes the human mano.
