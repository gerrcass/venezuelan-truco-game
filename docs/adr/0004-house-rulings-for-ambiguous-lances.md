# House rulings for ambiguous lances

Venezuelan truco varies by table, and the sources disagree on flor reservada, flor-envido betting, prive and several tie-breaks. Rather than hide those choices in code, the mesa adopts a written ruleset (`docs/rules/TRUCO-VENEZOLANO.md`): 24 piedras; flor reservada worth 5; both flors compared automatically (no flor-envido subgame); flor por derecho within the reparto; prive worth 1 at 23 and replacing envido; quemar supported; `a ley` and `barajo` flavor only; envido only on your own turn before the first card; the player who led a tied baza leads next. The prive and quemar are kept because they are distinctive Venezuelan texture, not because they are common.

**Consequences:** the rules modal must cite the sources and the house rulings, so players are never surprised by a table convention. Any future ruleset variant needs a new ADR rather than a silent code change.
