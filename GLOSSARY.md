# Truco e' Piedra

A single-player Venezuelan Truco game set at a Caracas kitchen table. Four players, two fixed pairs, 24 piedras, a lot of viveza criolla.

## Language

### The table

**Partida**:
The whole match, played to 24 piedras.
_Avoid_: Game, juego (ambiguous with "vale juego")

**Mesa**:
The table and, by extension, the social occasion of playing. A mesa has a mood.
_Avoid_: Room, lobby

**Reparto**:
One deal: four hands of three cards, a vira, three bazas and their cantos.
_Avoid_: Mano (for the deal), round

**Mano**:
The player who acts first in the reparto and wins ties. The mano rotates with the pie.
_Avoid_: First player, dealer

**Pie**:
The dealer of the reparto; plays last in the first baza.
_Avoid_: Repartidor, dealer

**Baza**:
One pass of four cards, one from each player. A reparto has up to three.
_Avoid_: Ronda, trick, round

**Parda**:
A baza where the highest cards tie. The first team to win a baza wins the reparto if the tie leaves no outright winner.
_Avoid_: Empate, draw

**Piedra**:
A match point. A partida is won at 24 piedras.
_Avoid_: Punto, point, stone

**Tanto**:
The numeric value of an envido or flor play, from 0 to 43.
_Avoid_: Puntos (when the number is an envido value)

### Cards

**Palo**:
A suit: oro, copa, espada or basto.
_Avoid_: Pinta (except in quoted table talk)

**Vira**:
The face-up card under the deck whose palo names the perico and perica.
_Avoid_: Muestra, turn-up

**Perico**:
The caballo of the vira's palo, or the rey if the vira is a caballo. The highest card in the game.
_Avoid_: Macho, jack

**Perica**:
The sota of the vira's palo, or the rey if the vira is a sota. The second-highest card.
_Avoid_: Hembra, ten

**Matas**:
The four fixed high cards: espadilla, bastillo, siete de espada and siete de oro.
_Avoid_: High cards, honors

**Espadilla**:
The as de espada. Third-highest card in the game.
_Avoid_: Ace of spades

**Bastillo**:
The as de basto. Fourth-highest card in the game.
_Avoid_: Ace of clubs

**Quemar**:
Playing a card face down. A burned card loses to every face-up card and ties only another burned card.
_Avoid_: Discard, fold

### Cantos

**Canto**:
A spoken bet or declaration: truco, envido, flor, prive.
_Avoid_: Bid, announcement

**Envido**:
A bet comparing the best tanto of each team, built from two cards of one palo or a piece. Only before the first card of the reparto.
_Avoid_: Envite, enviste (table variants)

**Flor**:
Three cards of one palo, or a piece plus two cards of one palo. Scores 3 piedras and cancels envido.
_Avoid_: Flower, flush

**Flor reservada**:
Perico and perica in the same hand. Scores 5 piedras automatically and beats any other flor.
_Avoid_: Royal flor, special flor

**Flor por derecho**:
The obligation to declare flor before playing your first card. This mesa plays flor por derecho.
_Avoid_: Optional flor

**Prive**:
A one-piedra envido comparison available when a team is one piedra from winning.
_Avoid_: Prize, private

**Truco**:
The bet that raises the reparto to 3 piedras, or the name of the whole game.
_Avoid_: Trick call

**Retruco**:
The raise of truco to 6 piedras.
_Avoid_: Re-truco, double truco

**Vale nueve**:
The raise of retruco to 9 piedras.
_Avoid_: Vale 9

**Vale juego**:
The final raise: the winner of the reparto wins the partida. If declined, the caller takes 9 piedras.
_Avoid_: Match bet

**Quiero**:
"I accept" a canto.
_Avoid_: Accept, yes

**No quiero**:
"I decline" a canto. The caller takes the previous stake.
_Avoid_: Reject, pass

**Irse al mazo**:
Folding the reparto. The opposing team takes the current stake.
_Avoid_: Fold, surrender

**Son buenas**:
"they're good": conceding an envido without revealing the tanto. Flavor in this game.
_Avoid_: Good game

**A ley**:
A table bluff suggesting you may or may not have flor. Flavor in this game, not a mechanic.
_Avoid_: Bluff call

**Barajo**:
The cry for a misdeal or a exposed piece. Flavor in this game, not a mechanic.
_Avoid_: Misdeal

### The people

**Pana**:
Your teammate and lifelong friend. Sends señas, talks trash, never lets you forget a bad card.
_Avoid_: Partner, ally

**Comadre**:
The rival who knows everyone's business and narrates every hand as if it were a telenovela.
_Avoid_: Opponent, rival

**Catire**:
The cocky rival who calls truco before looking at his cards.
_Avoid_: Opponent, rival

**Seña**:
A gesture a teammate uses to reveal one strong card or a flor. Each reparto gives you one.
_Avoid_: Hint, tell

**Viveza criolla**:
Venezuelan street smarts: winning with the cards you have, the face you make and the story you tell.
_Avoid_: Cheating, cunning
